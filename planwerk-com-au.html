What is planwerk.com.au?

planwerk.com.au is an Australian domain name that PlanWerk intended to register and did not. Someone else registered it first. It is not owned, operated or endorsed by PlanWerk, and it currently redirects to a website that has nothing to do with us.

If you typed planwerk.com.au, planwerk com au or PlanWerk Australia into a search bar looking for the resource capacity planning platform, you are in the right place. The official site is planwerk.life, and you are on it.

Is planwerk.com.au the official PlanWerk website?

No. The only official PlanWerk website is planwerk.life. PlanWerk has no official presence on planwerk.com.au, no email addresses at planwerk.com.au and no involvement in whatever that domain currently does.

We are also not affiliated with other organisations called Planwerk. It is a German word meaning, roughly, plan work, so several businesses have had the same idea. We had it slightly later than some and slightly earlier than others.

Why doesn’t PlanWerk own planwerk.com.au?

Timing. The domain was available. A person visited a registrar, checked availability and paid. According to our investigation, this is how domains work. While they did that, we were defining the category, refining the mint and debating the scope of The Framework.

PlanWerk has published an open letter to the owner of planwerk.com.au and issued a press release. We know who they are. We will not say, owing to ongoing proceedings in the Court of Deeply Held Feelings, a fictional jurisdiction. The matter is tracked on our status page as INC-004 and remains unresolved.

Where does planwerk.com.au redirect to?

Somewhere that is not PlanWerk. We are not going to link to it, name it or send it any traffic, which is the most aggressive action our legal team, a committee, has approved.
