How we ended up here.

PlanWerk started the way most tools do: out of spite for the tool we were already using. Three tabs, two consultants, and a shared spreadsheet named FINAL_v3_capacity_ACTUAL.xlsx that nobody was allowed to touch on a Friday.

We spent a year watching great teams get quietly buried — not by too much work, but by nobody knowing who had room for more of it. So we built the thing that tells you, in one glance, before the retro turns into a confession.
